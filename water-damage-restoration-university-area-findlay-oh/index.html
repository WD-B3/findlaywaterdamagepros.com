Water Damage Restoration Near the University of Findlay

Rental houses, shared housing and older homes near campus have their own water-damage problems. This page covers the first steps and who to tell.

☎ (567) 406-9153

The campus area

The University of Findlay lists its main address as 1000 N. Main Street in Findlay. The campus includes landmarks such as the Griffith Memorial Arch, dedicated in November 1923, and Old Main, both near the entrance.

The streets around a campus tend to mix owner-occupied homes with houses rented to students and staff. This page uses the area around the campus as a practical service reference, not as an official neighborhood boundary.

Why rental and shared housing is different

In a rental, the person who finds the water is often not the person who owns the building. Tenants usually need to tell the landlord or property manager quickly, and the owner decides on repairs, access and any insurance claim.

Shared houses add another wrinkle: several people may have belongings in the affected rooms, and not everyone is home when a leak starts. A fast, written note of what was wet and when it was found helps everyone later.

Tenant checklist

Shut off the water at the fixture or main valve if you can reach it safely, tell the landlord or manager right away, and photograph damage and belongings. Do not use outlets or switches near standing water, and leave if the ceiling is sagging.

Typical causes in older homes and rentals

  • Washing machine and dishwasher hoses that split or loosen.
  • Water heater leaks in basements and utility closets.
  • Frozen or burst pipes in unheated or poorly insulated spaces during winter.
  • Roof or window leaks that show up first as stained ceilings.
  • Basement seepage after heavy rain.

These are general causes in houses of this kind and not a record of events near the university. Neighborhood-specific FEMA flood-zone status not verified.

Which service fits

A leaking appliance or hose is covered under appliance leak water damage, and a failed water heater under water heater leak cleanup. If a pipe froze and split, see frozen pipe water damage.

Standing water in a lower level calls for basement water damage cleanup, and soaked carpet is handled in carpet water damage restoration. For the whole city, start at the Findlay water damage restoration home page.

Before you call from a rental

Address and unit

Give the street address and which unit or room is wet.

Landlord contact

Say whether the landlord or manager has been told, and whether they have approved work.

Water source

Say where the water came from and whether it is shut off.

Belongings

Move electronics and papers above the water line if it is safe, and photograph everything first.

Insurance and renter's coverage

A landlord's policy typically covers the building, while a renter's policy, if you have one, covers your belongings. Whether either pays depends on the policy and the cause of the loss. Nothing here promises coverage, so ask the insurer and keep photos, receipts and written messages.

Drying and mold

Wet carpet, pad and drywall that stay damp for days can grow mold, and a closed-up rental can hide it. Prompt extraction and drying reduce the risk, but no one can guarantee mold will not appear. Mention any musty smell or visible growth when you call.

University of Findlay area water damage questions

Is this an official University of Findlay service?

No. Findlay Water Damage Pros is an independent water damage service. It is not operated by, endorsed by or affiliated with the University of Findlay. The page only uses the campus area as a local reference.

I rent near campus. Who do I call first?

Tell your landlord or property manager right away and, if you can reach it safely, shut off the water. Then call the number on this page for guidance on extraction and drying. The owner decides on repairs and any claim.

Does a renter's policy cover water damage?

It can cover your belongings in some situations, depending on the policy and the cause. This site cannot promise coverage, so read the policy and ask the insurer.

Is it safe to stay in the house after a leak?

Not always. Avoid rooms with standing water near outlets or appliances, do not operate breakers while standing in water, and leave if ceilings sag. If sewage is involved, keep away and call for help.

Can water damage in a shared house lead to mold?

It can if wet materials stay damp. Quick drying lowers the risk, but nothing guarantees against mold. Tell dispatch if you notice a musty smell or visible growth.
