Before the crew arrives in McComb

Take these steps while dispatch arranges a crew.

Close the valve

Shut off the main or the nearest isolation valve. In shared buildings, tell the neighbor or landlord at once.

Keep power safe

Leave power off in wet rooms if the panel is dry. If water is near the panel, call the utility.

Protect and photograph

Lift valuables. Photograph the damage before moving anything.

What to do before help arrives in McComb

  • Find the source and close the nearest shutoff, whether it is a fixture valve or the main.
  • If the building is attached to a neighbor, tell them so they can check their side of the shared wall.
  • Take a short video that pans from the source to the farthest wet spot.
  • Give dispatch the address, the building type and who else is on the wall.

Industry practice is set out in ANSI/IICRC S500, the published standard for professional water damage restoration. A crew should be able to tell you how it applies to your building.
