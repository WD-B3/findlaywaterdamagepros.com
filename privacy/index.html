Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information we handle when you visit this site or call our line. Last updated November 9, 2026.

Who we are

Findlay Water Damage Pros operates this website at findlaywaterdamagepros.com and a phone line for water damage in Findlay, Ohio. Our address is 1800 Tiffin Ave, Findlay, OH 45840, and you can reach us at contact@findlaywaterdamagepros.com.

Information you give us

This site has no contact form, so it does not collect personal details through one. If you call, you may give us your name, phone number, address and a description of the water damage so dispatch can arrange help. If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in the message.

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes. Tell dispatch at the start of the call if you do not wish to be recorded.

How we use information

  • To respond to your call or email.
  • To arrange service at your address.
  • To improve the quality of our dispatch and training.
  • To meet legal or insurance record requirements when a job is performed.

Sharing

We share job details with the crews and service providers needed to help you. We may disclose information when the law requires it. We do not sell your personal information.

Website data

Like most websites, the host that serves this site may log basic technical information, such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested, to keep the site running and secure. This site does not use advertising trackers of its own.

Phone links

Tapping a phone number on this site opens your device's dialer. The call is then handled by your carrier and our line, and your carrier has its own policies.

Retention and your choices

We keep job records as long as needed for the purposes above and to meet legal obligations. You may ask what we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it where the law allows, by emailing contact@findlaywaterdamagepros.com.

Children

This site is intended for adults who own or manage property. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.

Questions about this policy?

Email contact@findlaywaterdamagepros.com, or call the 24/7 line if you have water in the building.
