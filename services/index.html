Water damage is not one problem. A split supply line, a flooded basement, a failed water heater and a sewer backup all start with water on the floor, but each one needs a different plan. The category of water, the materials it reached and how long it has been there decide what a crew removes, what it dries and what it rebuilds.

Professional practice for all of them follows the published ANSI/IICRC S500 standard. Below are the services we cover, grouped by cause. Each page explains the typical steps, the factors that change the work and the questions worth asking. If you are not sure which one fits, call the 24/7 line and describe what you see. Dispatch will tell you which service applies.
