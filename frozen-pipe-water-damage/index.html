Emergency Frozen Pipe Water Damage in Findlay, OH

Frozen pipes often do their damage after the thaw. This page covers what to shut off, how crews find hidden cracks and how drying works after a freeze in Findlay.

☎ (567) 406-9153

Frozen pipe water damage restoration usually starts after the ice melts, not while it is frozen. Ice expands inside a pipe, and the crack it opens stays plugged until the temperature rises, then the line leaks or sprays inside a wall. If you see frost on a pipe or hear water that suddenly starts running, call the 24/7 line in Findlay and shut off the main valve while you wait.

Frozen pipe emergency: what is urgent and what is not

A pipe that is frozen but not yet leaking is a warning, not a flood. Keep the heat on, open cabinet doors under sinks and let a faucet drip. Do not use an open flame to thaw it. A pipe that has thawed and is leaking is a flood in progress, and that is the moment to call.

Calls at night and on weekends are common after a cold snap, and dispatch treats them the same as daytime calls. Same-day response is offered when crews are available.

Why Findlay winters matter here

NOAA's 1991 to 2020 normals give Findlay about 24 inches of snow in an average year and a record low of minus 21 degrees. A cold spell like that is hardest on lines in unheated spaces such as garages, crawl spaces, exterior walls and the area behind a kitchen sink on an outside wall.

No official figure counts how many Findlay homes lose a pipe in a given winter, so this page sticks to how freezes damage plumbing rather than how often.

How crews find and dry frozen pipe damage

Pipes that cracked from freezing often split along a seam far from where you notice water. A thermal camera and moisture meter trace the wet path back to the break. Wet insulation and drywall are removed, standing water is extracted and drying equipment runs until the readings come down.

Cold weather complicates drying. Dehumidifiers work poorly in a freezing room, so crews often restore heat to the space as part of the job. That is one reason a winter water loss can take longer than the same loss in July.

Frozen Pipe Water Damage questions answered

Do you handle frozen pipe emergencies at night or on weekends in Findlay?

Yes. The dispatch line answers 24/7, and cold-snap calls on nights and weekends are treated as urgent. Same-day response is offered when crews are available.

Is it safe to thaw a frozen pipe myself?

A hair dryer or warm towels on an exposed pipe are common methods, never an open flame. If the pipe is inside a wall or already cracked, thawing it can release water, so turn off the main valve first.

How do I know a pipe cracked when it froze?

Watch for a drop in water pressure, wet drywall, stains that appear after a thaw and the sound of running water with every fixture off. A crack often shows up only when the ice melts.

Will insurance cover frozen pipe damage?

Frequently when the loss is sudden, but policies vary on heat requirements and on vacant homes. Ask your insurer and keep records.

Can drying happen in cold weather?

Yes, but the space usually needs heat, because dehumidifiers and drying slow down in cold air. The crew will tell you what temperature the job needs.
