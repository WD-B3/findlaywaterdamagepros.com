What to do before help arrives in Arlington

  • Close the valve nearest the leak, or the main shutoff if you cannot tell the source.
  • Keep clear of wet outlets, and leave breaker decisions to someone standing on dry ground.
  • Write the time you found the water on a sticky note and photograph it beside the damage.
  • Have a street address and a nearby cross street ready for dispatch.

The ANSI/IICRC S500 standard, which the trade uses as its reference, divides water into three categories by contamination. Dispatch will ask which one you are looking at.
