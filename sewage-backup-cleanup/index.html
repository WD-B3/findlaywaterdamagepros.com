Emergency Sewage Backup Cleanup in Findlay, OH

Raw sewage coming up a floor drain or toilet is a safety problem first. This page explains what to do, what Category 3 means and how professional cleanup works in Findlay.

Sewage backup: what to do first

Sewage backup cleanup in Findlay starts with keeping people and pets away from the water. Do not touch it, do not run fixtures that drain into the line and do not try to vacuum it with a household vacuum. Call the 24/7 dispatch line, and if the backup appears to be in the street main, call the city sewer utility as well.

Electricity and sewage

If sewage covers outlets, appliances or a panel, leave the area and call the electric utility or 911 before you call a restoration line. Cut power only if the panel is dry and reachable.

Same-day response is offered when crews are available, and sewage calls are treated as urgent at every hour. A backup at night is still a backup, and contaminated water is better contained early.

Why sewage is different from other water damage

Sewage is Category 3 under the ANSI/IICRC S500 standard, which means grossly contaminated. It can carry bacteria and other contaminants, so the cleanup uses protective gear, containment and disinfectant, and porous materials that the water touched are removed rather than dried. EPA's guidance on mold cleanup tells homeowners to call a professional experienced with contaminated water for exactly this situation, and this page follows that advice.

That does not mean everything in the house is lost. Hard surfaces and framing can be cleaned and sanitized, while carpet, pad and low drywall that were soaked are usually removed. A crew sorts items on site and explains what goes and why.

Who is responsible: your line or the main?

A backup that affects only one drain, such as a basement floor drain, may point to a clog in your own line. If several fixtures back up at once, or neighbors have the same problem, the cause may be the municipal main.

The city of Findlay runs its own wastewater system, and it also provides wastewater service to Arcadia and water and wastewater to Van Buren. Ask the utility first, and keep a record of when you called.

How sewage cleanup is done

The crew isolates the area, pumps out the contaminated water, removes affected porous materials, cleans and disinfects surfaces and dries the structure with air movers and dehumidifiers. Air scrubbers may be used in a contained space. Moisture readings confirm the drying, and the plumber or utility fixes the cause.

Sewage Backup Cleanup questions answered

Do you handle sewage backup emergencies at night or on weekends in Findlay?

Yes. The dispatch line is staffed 24/7 and sewage backups are treated as urgent. Same-day response is offered when crews are available.

Is it safe to clean up sewage myself?

EPA advises calling a professional experienced with contaminated water. If you must act, wear protective gear and keep children and pets out, but a crew is the safer choice.

Does homeowners insurance cover sewer backup?

Often only with a water backup endorsement. Ask your insurer.

How do I know whether it is my line or the city's?

If one drain backs up, it may be your line. If several fixtures or neighbors are affected, call the city sewer utility.

What happens to my belongings?

Hard goods can often be cleaned. Porous items like carpet, pad and upholstered furniture are usually discarded.
